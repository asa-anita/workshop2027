Register for ANITA 2027

One form covers the whole week, 8–12 February 2027: the AI for Astrophysics Summer School (Mon–Wed) and the 21st ANITA Science Workshop (Thu–Fri). Tell us which parts you will attend and we will ask only the questions that apply.

  • No fees: the school and the workshop are both free to attend.
  • Deadlines: summer school applications and workshop talk abstracts are due by Tuesday 1 December 2026, with decisions on Monday 7 December. The final workshop deadline is Monday 11 January 2027, when late talks and school waitlist placements are also settled.
  • Summer school places are limited to 30 and balanced across career stages, so a school registration is effectively an application. Places will be confirmed on Monday 7 December 2026 for applications received by 1 December. Later applications join a waitlist.
  • Workshop talks: as is traditional for an ANITA workshop, we are hoping to offer a talk to everyone who submits an abstract. Talks will be confirmed on Monday 7 December 2026 for abstracts received by 1 December. Later talks are considered as space allows.
  • Questions? Email dcroton@swin.edu.au.
