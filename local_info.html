Getting there

Local information

The school and the workshop are both held at Swinburne University of Technology in Melbourne.

Local details are still being finalised. Items marked TBC will be updated closer to the event.

Venue

Both the school and the workshop are held in the Swinburne Virtual Universe, Swinburne’s immersive 3D visualisation facility:

Room ATC103, Advanced Technologies Centre (ATC)
Swinburne University of Technology, Hawthorn campus
427–451 Burwood Road, Hawthorn VIC 3122

The ATC is on the Burwood Road side of the campus, a few minutes’ walk from Glenferrie station. See the Hawthorn campus map (PDF; the ATC is at grid D3) and the Hawthorn campus page.

If you have any accessibility requirements or concerns, please tell us in your registration or email dcroton@swin.edu.au.

Getting there

  • Train: Glenferrie station (Lilydale and Belgrave lines) is right next to the campus.
  • Tram: route 16 runs along Glenferrie Road past the campus (stop 74, Glenferrie Station). See the route 16 guide; routes 48, 70, 72, 75 and 109 also serve the area.
  • Plan your trip: use the Transport Victoria journey planner for times and connections, and see tickets and payments for myki and fares.
  • From the airport: take the SkyBus Melbourne City Express to Southern Cross station (timetables), then a train towards Lilydale or Belgrave to Glenferrie.
  • Swinburne’s getting to the Hawthorn campus page has more detail, including parking.

Accommodation

Participants book their own accommodation. These options are within walking distance of the venue or a short trip away; distances are approximate walking routes to the ATC. The Melbourne CBD is also an easy train ride from Glenferrie station.

The Glenferrie Hotel

Boutique pub hotel with en-suite rooms above a bar and restaurant. The closest hotel to the venue.

324 Burwood Road, Hawthorn – about 450 m, a 6 minute walk. Opposite Glenferrie station.

MainStay Suites Hawthorn

Serviced studios and one- and two-bedroom apartments with kitchens; good for longer stays or sharing. Formerly Quest Hawthorn.

616 Glenferrie Road, Hawthorn – about 400 m, a 6 minute walk.

Camberwell Serviced Apartment Hotel

Four-star serviced apartments, from studios to three bedrooms, in a quiet residential street.

85 Camberwell Road, Hawthorn East – about 1.5 km, a 20 minute walk.

The Sebel Melbourne Kew

Full-service hotel and serviced apartments; the most upmarket option nearby.

115 Cotham Road, Kew – about 2.2 km, a 30 minute walk, or tram 16 along Glenferrie Road.

Melbourne Kew Central Apartment Hotel

Simple, lower-cost apartment hotel near the shops and cafes of Kew Junction.

7 Studley Park Road, Kew – about 2.6 km, a 35 minute walk; use the journey planner for tram connections.
